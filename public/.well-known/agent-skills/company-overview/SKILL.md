# CloudTopia — Company Overview

CloudTopia is a Gulf-first, bilingual (Arabic + English) digital, cloud, and AI
agency founded in 2024 by Mohamad Shahm (محمد شهم), Founder & Lead Engineer
(Information Systems Engineer). It builds websites, e-commerce, web & mobile
apps, business systems (CRM/ERP), cloud infrastructure, and practical AI — with
scope and pricing agreed in a written proposal before the build, and full
ownership (code, accounts, analytics, docs) handed to the client.

"The cloud" means the internet itself — CloudTopia helps businesses get there,
operate there, and thrive there.

## Where it operates
- **Türkiye hub** — Turkey and the Levant (Iraq, Syria, Jordan, Lebanon)
- **Oman hub** — Oman and the Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain)
- Also serves Egypt and the wider Arab region.
