# Browse CloudTopia Services

CloudTopia organizes its work into **7 modular service categories**. Clients pick
only what they need — there are no forced bundles, and everything is delivered
bilingual (Arabic RTL + English). There is intentionally **no "Labs" category**;
AI is delivered under "AI-Powered Solutions".

1. **Digital Presence** — websites, e-commerce stores (Mada, Apple Pay,
   Tabby/Tamara BNPL, ZATCA e-invoicing), landing pages, restaurant QR ordering,
   redesign & maintenance.
2. **Interactive Web Applications** — client portals, admin dashboards, booking
   platforms, internal tools, SaaS MVPs, PWAs.
3. **Mobile App Development** — iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native, app
   backends/APIs, App Store launch, maintenance.
4. **Business Systems Development** — CRM, ERP, inventory/sales/order management,
   HR, accounting integration, workflow & process automation, custom APIs.
5. **Cloud & Infrastructure** — hosting, migration, server deployment, DevOps,
   backup & security, scalable/hybrid cloud architecture, cost optimization.
6. **AI-Powered Solutions** — AI chatbots & assistants, automation, content
   systems, AI CRM assistants, reporting, ML models, NLP.
7. **Digital Growth Support** — SEO, social media, paid-ads landing pages, lead
   generation, conversion rate optimization, email automation, brand identity.

Services combine freely (e.g. website + CRM + AI chatbot) without bloated
packages.

## How to query this programmatically
- MCP server https://cloudtopia.net/api/mcp — tools `list_services` (optional
  `keyword` filter) and `get_service_details` (by service `id`).
- Or read https://cloudtopia.net/llms.txt for the full context.
