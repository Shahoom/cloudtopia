# CloudTopia FAQ

## Do you build websites?

Yes. CloudTopia builds professional websites, landing pages, company websites, service websites, and SEO-ready pages.

## Do you build websites in Arabic and English?

Yes. CloudTopia can build Arabic, English, and multilingual websites with proper RTL support.

## Do you build CRM systems?

Yes. CloudTopia can build custom CRM systems for lead management, clients, follow-ups, sales pipelines, notes, and reporting.

## Do you build ERP systems?

Yes. CloudTopia can build business system workflows such as inventory, sales, invoices, clients, orders, and reporting depending on the company needs.

## Do you build dashboards?

Yes. CloudTopia builds dashboards, admin panels, portals, and internal tools.

## Do you connect WhatsApp?

Yes. CloudTopia can add WhatsApp buttons, structured WhatsApp lead messages, and in advanced cases WhatsApp Business API integration.

## Do you provide AI automation?

Yes. CloudTopia Labs works on AI assistants, AI chatbots, automation workflows, and smart internal tools.

## Can I manage website content myself?

Yes, if CMS/admin panel is included in the project scope.

## How do I start?

The best step is to share your business type, country, required service, and main goal. Then CloudTopia can suggest the right solution.
