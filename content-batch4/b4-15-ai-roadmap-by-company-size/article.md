## قيّم الجاهزية في ثلاثة محاور

أولاً البيانات: هل توجد في نظام معروف؟ هل يمكن الوصول إليها قانونياً وتقنياً؟ هل هي كاملة ومحدثة ومصنفة؟ ثانياً العملية: هل خطواتها مفهومة ومتكررة، أم تعتمد على استثناءات وخبرة فردية غير موثقة؟ ثالثاً الفريق: من يملك النتيجة، ومن يراجع المخرجات، ومن يستطيع تعديل الإجراء بعد الإطلاق؟

استخدم مقياساً بسيطاً من منخفض إلى مرتفع لكل محور، ولا تجمعه في رقم يخفي السبب. مشروع خدمة عملاء بقاعدة معرفة جيدة وفريق مسؤول قد يكون جاهزاً، بينما التنبؤ بالمبيعات على بيانات متفرقة ليس كذلك. الجاهزية لا تعني الكمال؛ تعني أن النقص معروف ويمكن معالجته داخل نطاق التجربة بدلاً من اكتشافه بعد الاستثمار.

Start with one 90-day project: a repeated problem, available data, a named owner, and a before-and-after measure. An AI roadmap for business is not a shopping list. It selects a decision or task that can improve and creates a controlled way to measure the change. Small companies need speed and bounded cost. Midsize firms need connections to operational systems. Enterprises need authorization, governance, and coordination across business units. ## Score readiness across data, process, and people

First, data: does it live in a known system, can it be accessed lawfully and technically, and is it current, complete, and classified? Second, process: are steps understood and repeated, or dependent on undocumented individual judgment? Third, team: who owns the outcome, checks outputs, and can change the procedure after deployment?

Rate each area low, medium, or high, but do not combine the ratings into one score that hides the cause. A customer-service knowledge base with a responsible team may be ready; sales forecasting on fragmented history may not. Readiness does not mean perfect information. ## Measure value without misleading management

Choose one primary measure and two guardrails. The primary measure may be request completion time, cost per transaction, or first-contact resolution. Guardrails prevent false improvement: error, rework, complaints, information exposure, or review burden. Establish the baseline with the same method that will be used after deployment.

Message or user volume alone does not prove return. Employees may use an enjoyable tool frequently without financial or service impact. Sample outputs and review quality. Separate time saved by the system from effort transferred to another reviewer. Record assumptions, and require a minimum value threshold and maximum risk level before scaling.
