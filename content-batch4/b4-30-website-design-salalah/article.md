## ابدأ بنوع النشاط ومسار الحجز الحقيقي

يختلف موقع الفندق عن موقع شقة فندقية، وشركة تأجير السيارات عن منظم الجولات، والمطعم عن مزود تجربة خارجية. الفندق يحتاج أنواع غرف وسياسات وتوفراً وربما ربطاً بنظام إدارة. الشقق تحتاج وحدات ومرافق وقواعد إقامة، بينما يحتاج تأجير السيارات إلى فئات وتسلم وتسليم وتأمين واضح.

منظم الأنشطة يعرض المدة ونقطة الالتقاء وما يشمله السعر والقيود المرتبطة بالطقس. المطعم يحتاج منيو حياً وحجز طاولة أو طلباً مباشراً. اجلس مع الاستقبال والحجوزات والمحاسبة قبل التصميم، وسجل ما يحدث من أول استفسار إلى التأكيد والتعديل والإلغاء والاسترداد.

## Map the booking path for each business type

A hotel does not need the same flow as a serviced apartment operator, car hire company, activity provider, or restaurant. Hotels present room types, policies, and availability, often with property-management integration. Apartments explain units, facilities, occupancy, and stay rules. Vehicle rental needs categories, collection, return, and insurance terms.

An activity provider publishes timing, meeting point, inclusions, capacity, and weather conditions. A restaurant needs a current menu, location, and a reservation or ordering route. Before designing screens, follow a real enquiry from discovery through confirmation, amendment, cancellation, and refund with reservations, operations, and finance.

The interface should reflect that operating process. If availability is reviewed manually, do not present a calendar as live inventory. Where a reliable system exists, connect it through an appropriate interface. Accurate status is more valuable than a polished calendar that does not represent actual rooms, vehicles, or seats.

## Mobile is the primary point of sale

A visitor may arrive from search, maps, an advertisement, or WhatsApp. The page should quickly explain the offer, location, suitability, and next action. Keep booking visible without hiding essential content behind a pop-up.

Test on real phones and moderate connections. Compress photography, limit autoplay video and fonts, and make date, guest, and phone fields easy. Preserve entries after an error. Do not require an account before visitors inspect options.

Make the phone number tappable and generate a useful WhatsApp message containing the product, date, and party size. A blank “I want to book” message merely moves all information gathering onto the reservations team.

## Choose the right level of booking automation

There are three levels. A structured enquiry is reviewed before confirmation. An availability calendar uses a dependable source but may defer payment. A booking engine handles selection, payment, confirmation, and amendments under explicit policies. Automation is useful only when the team can operate it accurately.

Properties selling through several channels must prevent double booking. Define the inventory source, update timing, and response to connection failure. Confirmation needs a reference, price, policy, and contact route. Do not confirm a request before its commercial and payment conditions are satisfied.
