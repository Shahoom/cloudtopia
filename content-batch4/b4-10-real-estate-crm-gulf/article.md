### ما أفضل نظام CRM لمكاتب العقار؟

الأفضل هو الذي يربط كل استفسار بمصدره ووحداته ووسيطه ومعايناته ومتابعته، ويمنع التكرار ويعمل على الهاتف. CRM جاهز يكفي للمكتب القياسي، بينما يناسب المخصص الشركات التي تحتاج وحدات ومشاريع وعمولات وتكاملات خليجية عميقة.

### هل أحتاج CRM مخصصاً أم جاهزاً؟

اختر الجاهز إذا كانت العملية جهات اتصال وصفقات ومهام ويمكن لفريقك اتباع نموذجه. اختر المخصص إذا كانت الوحدات والمشاريع والمطابقة والتوزيع والعمولات والتجديدات والتكاملات جوهر العمل، وأصبحت الحلول الالتفافية تسبب أخطاء أو عملاً يدوياً متكرراً.

## What makes real estate CRM different

A contact and a deal stage are not enough. A broker needs the properties viewed, areas accepted, budget, viewing schedule, rejection reason, and next action. Management must prevent uncontrolled duplicate assignment, monitor response time, and distinguish a portal lead from a campaign that actually produces qualified opportunities.

Leasing introduces contract and renewal dates, payments, and maintenance. Off-plan sales add developments, phases, unit plans, and reservation states. Commission rules need approvals and audit history rather than a field anybody can edit. Map the process from enquiry to closing before selecting software. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best CRM for a real estate agency?

The best system connects every enquiry to its source, properties, broker, viewings, and follow-up while preventing duplicates and working well on mobile. Ready-made CRM suits a standard agency; custom CRM fits firms that need developments, complex matching, commissions, and deep Gulf integrations.

### Can CRM connect to property portals?

Yes, when the portal provides an approved API, webhook, or structured lead-delivery method. Capabilities and terms differ. Test property matching, source attribution, duplicate handling, broker assignment, and error recovery. Do not scrape or collect data through a method prohibited by the portal.

### How much does real estate CRM cost?

Cost may combine SaaS seats and editions, or custom discovery, development, migration, hosting, support, and maintenance. It varies with users, lead sources, property records, WhatsApp, portals, contracts, and reports. Review current vendor pricing or request a detailed scope; one universal figure would be misleading.

### Do I need custom or ready-made CRM?

Choose ready-made CRM when contacts, deals, tasks, and standard automation describe the workflow. Choose custom when properties, developments, matching, assignment, commissions, renewals, and integrations are core operating logic, and workarounds are producing repeated manual work or unreliable data.
