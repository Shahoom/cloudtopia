## B. The 12 cross-verified findings (≥2 independent agents agree)
1. **Local Pack = 43% of clicks on "شركة + مدينة" queries and CloudTopia captures 0%** (no GBP). Reviews are the #1 Gulf trust signal (87–90% read them). → GBP + review flow is the single fastest lever, independent of DA.
2. **Commercial queries mostly escape AI Overviews** (8–18% trigger rate vs 35–65% informational) → the money queries are still classic SERPs; fight there.
3. **Zero-click is 68%+; #1 organic ≈ old #3.** Citation presence (snippets/PAA/AI answers) now > blue-link position.
4. **63% of AI citations go to listicles/tables; prose gets 4%.** Quotes +41%, stats +30–40%, and low-rank pages gain most (+115%). llms.txt: **zero measured citation impact** — keep, don't invest.
5. **Clutch + Foursquare feed AI "best agency" answers** (Foursquare ≈70% of ChatGPT local data). Profiles = cheap AI visibility.
6. **KSA searches 71% Arabic; UAE flips English-heavy; buyers code-switch** ("ويب ديزاين"). Arabic is <3% of web content vs its demand → Arabic depth = structural advantage.
7. **Gulf buyers verify CR/registry + map address before contacting.** Entity proof (CR, VAT, address, schema `sameAs`) is a moat none of the 13 cross-border competitors can fake (they leak +20/+90/+1 phones and USD pricing).
8. **GCC competitor field splits hard AR-only vs EN-only — nobody is strong bilingual.** (12 mapped: Geexar/Method/Rekaz/Topline/Efadh KSA · WebCastle/CanadaHitech UAE · Tocaan KW · ExpertWeb QA · Mazoon/AlphaWeb/Mercury OM). AlphaWeb (Oman, EN-only, 27 reviews) is the closest analogue. AI/cloud is a bullet for all, product for none.
9. **CloudTopia is UNDERPRICED to the point of damaging credibility**: cross-platform app $2,999 vs Dubai floor AED 18–25k (≈10x under mid-market); SEO $199/mo vs Oman "under 100 OMR = no real work" line and Dubai SME cluster AED 3–8k. Websites tiers ($150/$299/$999) are correctly aggressive. USD framing reads "offshore".
10. **Prices exist in pricing.md (AI-readable) but the human /pricing page shows "custom quote" only** — verified contradiction. Also: QR page sells a "14-day free trial" whose buttons are WhatsApp links (no trial exists), "hundreds of restaurants" vs 1 portfolio cafe, public "3 VIEWS" counters on articles.
11. **Instagram category is uncontested** (top dedicated account 10.5K, median 2–3K, 2/9 dormant, Oman ≈empty); nobody uses wa.me bio links; winning format = objection-handling carousels (maps 1:1 to the 36 unpublished drafts). **Brand collision: Maybelline "Cloudtopia" saturates the term/hashtag** — optimize intent hashtags, never #cloudtopia.
12. **Article template rules** (behavior research): hook ≤60 words answer-first · subhead every 150–200 words · inline CTA at 40–50% scroll (only 20–55% reach the end) · AR body 18–20px/1.6–1.8 line-height, top-RIGHT emphasis · titles: number/year/bracket, front-load 40 chars, write AR titles independently.
