## Where Wix breaks for Arabic

1. **RTL is surface-level.** `dir="rtl"` on body, but many Wix theme elements don't mirror properly (sliders, footers, some form layouts).
2. **Typography is limited.** You can't easily load IBM Plex Arabic or Amiri. Wix's built-in Arabic font options are minimal.
3. **Arabic SEO is constrained.** URL slugs, hreflang, and bilingual sitemap structure are harder. You can't easily have native Arabic-script URLs.
4. **Performance ceiling.** Wix sites typically score 60–75 on mobile Lighthouse — a custom Next.js site scores 95+. For Arabic ecommerce or content ranking, performance is a direct ranking factor.
5. **No deep customization.** Need a QR menu, a booking form tied to a specific flow, or a calculator? Wix says "use our app" which is usually English-first and not RTL.

## Where Wix is fine

- A personal portfolio that's mostly images.
- A 3-page landing for an idea you're testing before committing.
- An event page that'll be live for 6 weeks.

For anything that represents a real business long-term, the Wix rent adds up and the Arabic ceiling hurts.
