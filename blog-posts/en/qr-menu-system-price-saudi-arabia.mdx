"How much does a QR menu system cost in Saudi Arabia?" gets answers ranging from "SAR 200" (it's a PDF) to "SAR 50,000" (multi-branch enterprise). The honest range for something actually good: **SAR 800 to SAR 15,000** depending on scope.

<Callout tone="info" title="Short answer">
SAR 800–2,500 for a browse-only bilingual QR menu. SAR 3,000–6,000 with order-to-kitchen functionality. SAR 5,000–15,000 with full order-and-pay + ZATCA receipts. Foodics/Marn SaaS: SAR 300–800/month. PDF on Drive: SAR 0 (and worth it).
</Callout>

## Where restaurants get upcharged

- **Per-branch monthly fees** on SaaS platforms that stack up fast.
- **Menu update fees** — if the provider charges SAR 200 per menu change, you'll pay hundreds over a year.
- **"Add Mada" as an upsell** when it should be included from day one.
- **Transaction commissions** on top of the payment gateway fee.
- **Lock-in contracts** — annual commitments for something you could have custom-built.

## What should be included at SAR 5,000

- Bilingual (AR + EN) menu with photos and categories.
- Ordering flow with cart and modifier options.
- Mada, Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard integration.
- Kitchen printer setup (Star/Epson).
- ZATCA-compliant e-receipt generation.
- Admin dashboard for the owner/manager.
- Staff training for 1–2 hours.
- 30 days of free fixes post-launch.

If your quote for SAR 5,000 is missing any of these, ask why.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Custom vs SaaS decision">
At 1 location, SaaS (Foodics, Marn) often wins on simplicity. At 3+ locations, custom pays off — you avoid per-branch monthly fees that add up to SAR 15,000+/year.
</Callout>

<FAQ title="Common questions about QR menu system pricing in Saudi Arabia">
  <Q q="Can I get a QR menu for under SAR 500?">
    Technically yes — a PDF on Drive. Not recommended for a real business. SAR 800 is the honest floor for something customers won't hate.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Is Foodics worth SAR 300–800/month?">
    For POS-native chain operations, yes. For a single-location café that just needs a menu, no — you're paying for features you won't use.
  </Q>
  <Q q="How long does a QR menu take to build?">
    1–2 weeks for browse-only. 3–4 weeks with ordering. 5–8 weeks for multi-branch with POS integration.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Do I need a ZATCA-compliant receipt for QR menu orders?">
    If you're VAT-registered and customers pay through the menu, yes. See our [ZATCA Phase 2 guide](/blog/zatca-phase-2-e-invoicing-developer-checklist).
  </Q>
  <Q q="Can I add my loyalty program to the QR menu?">
    Yes — at SAR 2,000–5,000 additional. Points, stamp cards, or tiered memberships all doable.
  </Q>
  <Q q="What's the ongoing cost?">
    Custom: hosting ~SAR 400/month, maintenance optional. SaaS: SAR 300–800/month per location.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Is multi-language (Arabic + English + other) important?">
    Arabic and English yes. A third language (Urdu, Filipino, French for tourist-heavy areas) is SAR 500–1,500 extra per language.
  </Q>
</FAQ>

SAR 800 buys a proper browse-only menu. SAR 5,000 buys ordering, payments, and ZATCA. SAR 15,000 buys enterprise multi-branch. Under SAR 800, you're paying for a PDF; over SAR 15,000 for a single location, you're overpaying.
