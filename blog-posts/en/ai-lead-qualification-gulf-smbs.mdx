## The workflow

1. **Lead enters** from WhatsApp, website form, Meta/Google Ads, or offline event.
2. **AI asks 2–4 qualifying questions** in the customer's language.
3. **Scoring combines:**
   - Explicit signals (budget, timeline, role).
   - Implicit signals (message quality, engagement depth).
   - Enrichment (email domain, company size estimate).
4. **Scored lead routes** to:
   - Hot → instant WhatsApp ping to sales + call.
   - Warm → scheduled nurture + weekly touch.
   - Cold → long-nurture email sequence.
5. **Human handover** when score is high or conversation requires.

## What scoring actually considers

- **Budget signal** — explicit mention of price range, or implicit from product interest.
- **Authority** — decision-maker or influencer.
- **Need** — urgency, problem specificity, timeline.
- **Timeline** — "this week" vs "next year".
- **Engagement quality** — detail in responses, question answers.

This is effectively a BANT/MEDDPICC frame implemented in prompts + rules.

## Typical ROI

A Gulf SMB receiving 500 leads/month with no qualification might convert 3% to meetings = 15 meetings. With AI qualification that filters out cold leads and routes the hot ones to sales within 2 minutes: 6–8% conversion = 30–40 meetings from the same lead volume. No extra ad spend, same team, 2× the pipeline.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Speed to lead matters more than the qualification itself">
The #1 ROI driver is responding within 2 minutes. Qualification is secondary. Build the AI workflow so the hot-lead response happens instantly — even if the conversation is 80% bot at first — and the human enters 30 seconds later.
</Callout>
