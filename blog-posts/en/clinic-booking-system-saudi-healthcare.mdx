## Core features

1. **Bilingual AR+EN** — from symptoms/department selection to confirmation.
2. **Specialty selection** — filter by specialty, doctor, language, gender.
3. **Calendar availability** — real-time with buffer management.
4. **SMS + WhatsApp reminders** — 24 hours + 1 hour before.
5. **Insurance eligibility** — Bupa, Tawuniya, MedGulf, etc.
6. **Online payment** — Mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay for consultation fees.
7. **Pre-appointment forms** — medical history, consent, COVID screening.
8. **Post-visit follow-up** — feedback form, lab result delivery, prescription renewal.
9. **Integration with doctor calendars** — Google Calendar, Outlook.
10. **Reporting dashboard** — no-show rate, utilization, revenue per doctor.

## Regulatory context

- **NHIES / Sehhaty** — optional but valuable linkage for personal health record continuity.
- **CBAHI accreditation** — your system should support audit-friendly logs.
- **ZATCA Phase 2** — if you're taxable and issue invoices. See [our ZATCA checklist](/en/blog/zatca-phase-2-e-invoicing-developer-checklist).
- **PDPL** — Saudi data privacy law applies to patient data. Use KSA or nearby region hosting.

## No-show reduction

Typical Saudi clinic no-show rate without reminders: 25–35%. With SMS + WhatsApp reminders + small confirmation step: 8–15%. That 15–20% gap is pure revenue recovery — often pays for the whole system in 3–6 months.

<Callout tone="tip" title="WhatsApp reminders beat SMS in KSA">
Open rates: WhatsApp ~90%, SMS ~60%. Send the reminder via WhatsApp when you have consent and the patient has WhatsApp. Fall back to SMS only if needed.
</Callout>
