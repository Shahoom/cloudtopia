## Core features

1. **Search with filters** — price, size, area, bedrooms, type, completion status.
2. **Map view** — visual search is essential in UAE.
3. **DLD / RERA data display** — permit numbers, DEWA/ADM info where required.
4. **Bilingual AR+EN** — full parity, not just menu.
5. **High-quality media** — photos, video tours, floor plans, 360° where available.
6. **Portal feed sync** — Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle. XML/JSON feeds in and out.
7. **Lead capture** — form, WhatsApp deep link, click-to-call.
8. **Agent profile pages** — each agent has a page with listings, contact, reviews.
9. **CRM integration** — HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or Property Finder's own.
10. **SEO** — per-listing pages indexed, city/area pages, hreflang.

## Lead capture that works

- **WhatsApp deep link** on every listing — the #1 preferred channel in UAE.
- **Click-to-call** with area-based routing to the right agent.
- **Short form** (name, phone, budget, timeframe) — 4 fields max.
- **Auto-response via WhatsApp/email** within 2 minutes — critical for conversion.
- **Retargeting pixel** on every listing view for paid media follow-up.

## Portal feed strategy

Don't choose between portals and your own site — do both. Feed your inventory to Bayut, Property Finder, and Dubizzle, and drive qualified search traffic to your own site via SEO and paid. Track which listings convert via which channel.

<Callout tone="tip" title="The 2-minute response rule">
UAE property leads convert 6× higher when contacted within 2 minutes of submission. Set up WhatsApp auto-responses with a real agent routing after the auto-reply. Set up SLA monitoring to catch misses.
</Callout>
