"How much does an ecommerce site cost in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?" gets a wide range of answers because UAE ecommerce involves local payment rails (Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, Network International, Telr), bilingual design, VAT-compliant invoicing, and logistics integrations that a generic Shopify quote never covers.

Here are the real AED ranges we see in 2026 and what each tier actually delivers.

<Callout tone="info" title="Short answer">
A professional bilingual ecommerce site in the UAE in 2026 costs **AED 5,000–30,000 one-time**. Basic catalog + payments: AED 5,000–12,000. Full ecommerce with BNPL, VAT-compliant invoicing, multi-branch: AED 15,000–30,000+. SaaS (Shopify/Salla/Zid) runs AED 150–2,000/month.
</Callout>

## What changes the price

1. **Payment stack.** Network International + Apple Pay + Samsung Pay + Tabby + Tamara adds AED 3,000–8,000 vs a single Stripe/PayPal setup.
2. **VAT invoicing.** UAE FTA VAT invoices with TRN, bilingual fields, and proper number sequencing = AED 2,000–6,000.
3. **Arabic RTL quality.** Real bilingual AR+EN adds 25–35% to design/dev vs a single-language site. See our [Arabic RTL design rules](/blog/arabic-rtl-web-design-rules).
4. **Multi-emirate logistics.** Integrations with Fetchr, Aramex, DHL, Quiqup, Careem Now — budget AED 1,500–4,000 per integration.
5. **.ae domain + hosting with low UAE latency.** AED 300–1,000/year.

## Hidden costs to ask about

- **Product photography** — AED 300–800/product for lifestyle shots.
- **Content writing (AR+EN)** — AED 3,000–12,000 for 20–50 products.
- **Ongoing hosting + maintenance** — AED 400–1,500/month.
- **Post-launch changes** — ask for the hourly rate in writing.
- **App/feature additions** — review scope monthly to avoid creep.

## When to choose SaaS vs custom in UAE

- **Under AED 500k/yr GMV and launching fast:** Shopify or Salla.
- **AED 500k–2M GMV:** start SaaS, plan migration as you scale.
- **Above AED 2M GMV:** custom pays for itself in 18 months.

<FAQ title="Common questions about UAE ecommerce website cost">
  <Q q="Is Stripe enough in the UAE?">
    Stripe works but misses Apple Pay/Samsung Pay optimization and doesn't handle Tabby or Tamara. For a serious UAE store you need Network International or Telr plus BNPL.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Do I need VAT-compliant invoicing from day one?">
    If you're VAT-registered (AED 375,000+ annual turnover), yes. Invoices must show TRN, VAT amount, bilingual fields, and be stored for at least 5 years.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Can I use a .com or do I need .ae?">
    Either works. .ae helps for local trust and some local SEO signals, but .com with hreflang is equally rankable. If you do pick .ae you'll need a UAE trade license for registration.
  </Q>
  <Q q="How long does a UAE ecommerce build take?">
    Starter: 3–5 weeks. Professional: 6–10 weeks. Premium with multi-branch: 10–16 weeks.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Which logistics partner is best?">
    Aramex for general parcels, Fetchr for same-day, Quiqup for premium same-hour, Careem Now for food. Most stores integrate 2–3.
  </Q>
  <Q q="Do I need an Arabic version?">
    Strongly recommended. UAE has 80%+ Arabic speakers at home, and Arabic SEO captures traffic English cannot. Bilingual adds 25–35% to budget but grows TAM significantly.
  </Q>
  <Q q="What about cash on delivery?">
    Still popular in UAE (~15–25% of orders depending on category). Every logistics integration should support COD with reconciliation into your admin dashboard.
  </Q>
</FAQ>

## The honest summary

A professional bilingual UAE ecommerce site costs AED 5,000–30,000 one-time, depending on scope. Under that range you're paying for a template. Above it, you're paying for agency overhead you rarely need.

Fix the scope, fix the price, and ask for year-one total in writing.
